Frequently Asked Questions

What is DrawWise?

DrawWise is an entertainment and statistical number-analysis application. It provides number-generation tools, selected draw information, and statistical insights.

Does DrawWise sell lottery tickets?

No. DrawWise does not sell lottery tickets, accept wagers, process gambling transactions, or provide monetary prizes.

Does DrawWise predict winning numbers?

No. Generated combinations, historical trends, frequency data, and other statistics do not predict future results or guarantee a win.

What is DrawWise Premium?

DrawWise Premium is an optional auto-renewable subscription that provides access to advanced historical statistics, number frequency and trend analysis, additional statistical insights, and an ad-free experience.

Why am I seeing advertisements?

Advertisements may be displayed to users of the free version of DrawWise. An active DrawWise Premium subscription provides an ad-free experience.

How do I restore my subscription?

Open DrawWise and use the “Restore Purchases” option available in the subscription or settings area. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions purchased through Apple are managed through your Apple Account. You can manage or cancel your subscription from the Subscriptions section of your Apple Account settings.

On iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions.

I purchased Premium but it is not showing.

First try Restore Purchases inside DrawWise. If the issue continues, contact DrawWise Support.

Can I delete my DrawWise account?

If account deletion is available inside the app, follow the account deletion option in Settings. If you experience problems, contact DrawWise Support.
