1. Information You Provide

Depending on the features you use, BokNova may process information you provide directly, such as:

  • Account information, such as an email address or account identifier
  • Optional profile details you choose to add, such as a display nickname or region
  • Saved number picks and app preferences
  • Support requests or messages you send to us

2. Information Generated Through App Use

BokNova may process information associated with the use of the app, including:

  • Generated number combinations
  • Saved picks
  • App settings
  • Subscription status
  • Basic app usage information needed to operate and improve the service

Historical draw results, number frequency data, and other statistical information shown in BokNova are general, publicly available data. This statistical data is not personal information and does not identify you.

3. Subscriptions and Purchases

BokNova Premium subscriptions are processed through Apple’s In-App Purchase system.

BokNova does not directly receive or store your full payment card information.

Apple may process purchase, subscription, billing, and transaction information according to Apple’s own privacy policies.

BokNova may receive information necessary to determine whether a subscription is active, expired, restored, or otherwise valid.

4. Advertising

The free version of BokNova may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob.

Depending on your device settings, consent choices, region, and the advertising configuration used by BokNova, third-party advertising providers may process information such as:

  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers, where permitted
  • IP address
  • Device and operating system information
  • Advertising interaction information
  • Approximate location derived from technical information, where applicable
  • Diagnostic and performance information

BokNova may use Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework where required.

If tracking permission is requested, users may choose whether to allow tracking.

Users can change tracking permissions in their iOS privacy settings.

Third-party services process information according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Policies
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

5. Analytics and Diagnostics

BokNova may use technical diagnostics or analytics to understand crashes, performance, and general app usage.

If BokNova uses specific analytics or crash-reporting services, those services will be identified in this section.

6. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate BokNova
  • Maintain user accounts
  • Save user preferences and picks
  • Verify subscription status
  • Provide customer support
  • Detect technical problems
  • Improve app stability and performance
  • Display advertising to free users
  • Comply with legal obligations

7. Sharing of Information

BokNova does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers only when necessary to operate features of the app, such as:

  • Apple for authentication, purchases, and subscriptions
  • Google AdMob for advertising
  • Hosting, database, authentication, analytics, or infrastructure providers actually used by BokNova

Service providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect legal rights, users, or the security of the service.

8. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, or fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Account-related data may be deleted or anonymized when an account is deleted, subject to legal, security, fraud-prevention, or technical retention requirements.

9. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their BokNova account and associated personal information through the account deletion feature in the app, where available, or by contacting:

support@boknova.app

We may need to retain limited information where required by law, security requirements, fraud prevention, or legitimate record-keeping obligations.

10. Children

BokNova is not intended for children.

The app provides content relating to lottery number information and is intended for users who satisfy the age requirements applicable to lottery participation in their jurisdiction.

BokNova does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

11. International Users

BokNova may be used in different countries and regions. Information may be processed in jurisdictions other than the user’s place of residence depending on the infrastructure and service providers used.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot promise absolute security.

13. Third-Party Links and Services

BokNova may contain links to third-party websites, services, or content. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

When material changes are made, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated.
